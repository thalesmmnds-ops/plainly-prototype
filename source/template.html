What changed

  1. The nav is Logo, Inventory, Dictionary, Sign in.
  2. The Dictionary is A to Z, with a short line on every row about what it is and does. The third verdict stays "Skip it".
  3. Tap any item to open a bigger image and a fuller description. Where that content doesn't exist yet, it is lorem ipsum.
  4. Inventory opens on What's left, with I have and All items one tap away. Only clothes-like items take a number: bodysuits, zip-up sleepers and the sleep sack (one count per age), then fitted crib sheets, burp cloths, bottles, pacifiers, swaddle, baby gates and furniture anchors (one count). Everything else is Have it or Not for us.
  5. The age tag starts on All. Picking an age changes only the three items that come in sizes.

To confirm

  • Towels aren't in the guide yet, so your "6 towels" example isn't here. The cpo-strategist proposes adding baby towels and washcloths as a counted item in the first batch of new entries.
  • Diapers and wipes are Have it only here. A count that falls every day goes stale and reads like a restock list.
  • The control is called "Baby's age" here. The cpo-strategist suggests "Size", because gifts often run ahead of the baby.
  • "You're covered" counts an item once you have it, by a tap or a count of 1 or more, whatever the count.
  • Signing in is faked. The sheet opens on your first change, and either button signs you in.
